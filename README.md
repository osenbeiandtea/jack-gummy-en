# 🎃 Jack Gummy

A tiny Halloween toy for your browser. Pinch a pumpkin gummy, gently bump it into one of a different color, and collect flavors. Soak up enough and it turns dark, grins, and bursts into stars. Then you get a one-of-a-kind **mystery-flavor gummy**, named after the flavors you collected.

**Play:** https://osenbeiandtea.github.io/jack-gummy-en/

Free, no login, no ads. It never asks for your name or email.

## How to play

- Pinch and drag a gummy with your finger or mouse.
- Bump it softly into a gummy of a different color to absorb a bit of its flavor.
- Keep going and see what you end up with. Every gummy comes out a little different.

Buttons at the top: sound on/off, start over, and credits.

## Requirements

A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) on phone or desktop. Best in portrait on a phone. No installation needed.
